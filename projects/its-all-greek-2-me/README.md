# It's All Greek 2 Me
**Vocabulary and etymology through interactive learning**

[Public app](https://itsallgreek2me.app) · [Portfolio overview](../../README.md)

## The problem
A vocabulary game needs engaging interactions and dependable learning content. A record can be structurally valid while still containing an inaccurate definition or a misleading explanation of a word's origin.

## My role
I direct product development with AI assistance. I define learning and gameplay requirements, review content, identify defects and guide iterative corrections and launch preparation.

## Approach
- Develop a mobile-friendly learning experience using React and TypeScript.
- Use Supabase/PostgreSQL to support application data.
- Review content meaning separately from structural consistency.
- Make changes in stages and validate affected behaviour before expanding scope.

## Selected product decision
Treat content quality as part of the product experience. A polished interface alone does not make a learning product useful: definitions, examples and word-origin explanations need their own review criteria.

## Validation approach
The development process combines content review with targeted checks of changed behaviour. Remaining launch work is tracked explicitly; an implemented feature and a verified release are treated as separate milestones.

## Relevant capabilities
Product requirements · content-data quality · React/TypeScript and Supabase through AI-assisted development · iterative testing · prioritization.

## Explore
The public app provides a view of the learning experience. The product remains under active development, and its application source and content collections remain private.
